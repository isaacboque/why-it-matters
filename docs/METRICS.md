# Metrics

Every number the product shows or claims, in two depths. Generated from `src/whyitmatters/pulse/glossary.py` (`python -m whyitmatters.pulse.glossary --write`); a test keeps this file in step. For each metric: **Fan** is the one-line meaning, **Analyst** the definition, **Why** why it matters, **?** the help text shown next to the number (English; Spanish and Catalan are in the glossary), **Used by** the insight kinds whose claims carry it. Formulas, sources and validation are in [METHODS.md](METHODS.md); the independent recomputation of each number is in [STATS-AUDIT.md](STATS-AUDIT.md).

| Metric | Fan | Analyst | Why it matters | ? | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Possession** (`possession_share`) | Who has had the ball. | Share of in-play seconds with the ball in the window; gaps over 20 s are dead ball. | Controlling the ball controls the tempo and keeps the opponent from attacking. | Share of the time each team had the ball. | `state_shift` |
| **Pass completion** (`pass_completion`) | How often passes find a teammate. | Completed passes divided by attempted passes in the window. | Clean passing is the sign of a team that is comfortable on the ball. | Share of passes that reached a teammate. | `state_shift` |
| **Field tilt** (`field_tilt`) | Who is playing in the other team's end. | A team's passes in the final third (x >= 70) divided by both teams' passes there. | A tilted field means one side lives in the other's half. | Share of the passes in the attacking third that each team made. | head-to-head only |
| **Turnovers per minute** (`turnovers_per_min`) | How often the ball changes hands. | Open-play losses (ball won by the opponent, or put out) per minute, both teams. | A high number means a loose, end-to-end game. | How many times the ball changes hands each minute. | `state_shift` |
| **Window** (`window_minutes`) | How long the numbers cover. | Length of the rolling window, in minutes. | Every live number says over how long it was measured. | The number of minutes a figure is measured over. | `every windowed kind` |
| **Shot xG** (`shot_xg`) | How good one chance was. | Expected goals of one shot, from distance and angle (and pressure). | It separates a tap-in from a speculative effort. | The chance of a shot becoming a goal, from where it was taken. | `goal`, `big_chance` |
| **Score (home)** (`score_home`) | The home goals so far. | Goals scored by the home side after the event. | The context for everything else. | Home goals so far. | `goal` |
| **Score (away)** (`score_away`) | The away goals so far. | Goals scored by the away side after the event. | The context for everything else. | Away goals so far. | `goal` |
| **Completed passes** (`player_completed_passes`) | How many passes a player has landed. | Completed passes by the player up to that event. | A busy passer is usually where the team's play runs through. | Passes that reached a teammate. | `pass_master` |
| **Carry speed** (`carry_speed_kmh`) | How fast a player ran with the ball. | Ball speed over a carry, km/h. | Pace with the ball breaks defensive lines. | Speed of a run with the ball, km/h. | `top_speed` |
| **Team xG** (`team_xg`) | How many goals a team's chances were worth. | Sum of the xG of the team's shots up to that event. | It says who deserved what, whatever the score. | Total expected goals of a team's shots. | `goal` |
| **Duels won, own third** (`duel_share_def`) | Who wins the fights near their own goal. | A team's duels won in its defensive third / (its wins + the opponent's wins on the same grass). | Winning duels at the back keeps the ball out of danger. | Share of the tackles and interceptions near a team's own goal that it won. | head-to-head only |
| **Duels won, midfield** (`duel_share_mid`) | Who wins the fights in the middle. | Duels won in the middle third / (own wins + the opponent's wins in the same third). | The side that wins midfield duels decides how the game flows. | Share of the tackles and interceptions in midfield that a team won. | `midfield_battle` |
| **Duels won, final third** (`duel_share_att`) | Who wins the fights near the opponent's goal. | Duels won in the attacking third / (own wins + the opponent's wins on the same grass). | It is the signature of a high press. | Share of the tackles and interceptions near the opponent's goal that a team won. | head-to-head only |
| **Midfield duels won** (`duels_won_mid`) | How many midfield fights a team won. | Tackles won and interceptions in the middle third (count). | A share is only meaningful over enough duels. | Number of midfield tackles and interceptions won. | `midfield_battle` |
| **High recoveries** (`high_recoveries`) | Times the ball was won back high up the pitch. | Tackles won and interceptions at x >= 52.5. | Winning the ball high puts the team close to goal with the defence stretched. | Times the ball was won back in the opponent's half. | `press_surge` |
| **Final-third recoveries** (`final_third_recoveries`) | Times the ball was won back near the opponent's goal. | Tackles won and interceptions at x >= 70. | These are the most dangerous turnovers a defence can concede. | Times the ball was won back in the attacking third. | head-to-head only |
| **Counter-press recoveries** (`counter_press`) | Winning it straight back after losing it. | Ball won back within 5 s of the same team losing it. | It stops the opponent's counter before it starts. | Balls won back within five seconds of losing them. | head-to-head only |
| **Losses in own half** (`own_half_losses`) | Giving the ball away close to your own goal. | Possession lost at x < 52.5. | Losses near your own goal are the ones that become shots against you. | Times a team lost the ball in its own half. | `danger_turnovers` |
| **Progressive passes** (`progressive_passes`) | Passes that move the ball well forward. | Completed passes of at least 10 m towards goal, or from outside into the final third. | A team that progresses the ball is making real headway, not just keeping it. | Completed passes that moved the ball at least 10 m towards goal. | head-to-head only |
| **Long-ball share** (`long_ball_share`) | How often a team goes long. | Passes of 30 m or more / all passes. | A jump in long balls is how a team answers a press. | Share of passes that travel 30 m or more. | `direct_switch` |
| **Central build-up share** (`central_buildup_share`) | Whether a team builds through the middle or out wide. | Build-up passes (x <= 63) started in the central third / all build-up passes. | Building through the middle is riskier and quicker. | Share of build-up passes that start in the middle of the pitch. | head-to-head only |
| **Passes per defensive action** (`ppda`) | How hard a team presses. | PPDA: opponent passes in their own 60 % per tackle, interception or foul by the team in its attacking 60 %. Lower means harder. | It is the standard single-number measure of pressing intensity. | How many passes a team lets the opponent make before it challenges. Lower means a harder press. | `press_surge`, `direct_switch`, `press_escape` |
| **Pressures** (`pressures`) | How often a team closes down the ball. | Pressure events by the team in the window. | Pressure volume shows effort; pressure success shows whether it paid off. | Times a team closed down the player with the ball. | head-to-head only |
| **Pass difficulty** (`pass_difficulty`) | How hard a pass was to pull off. | Difficulty index 0 to 1 of a completed pass, from the pass length, pressure on the passer and how far into the final third it goes. | It credits the passes that only good players complete. | How hard the pass was, from 0 (easy) to 1 (very hard). | `pass_difficulty` |
| **Shot speed** (`shot_speed_kmh`) | How hard a shot was hit. | Ball speed of a shot, km/h. | Power is a small part of a shot, but it is the part everyone sees. | Speed of the ball off the boot, km/h. | `shot_speed` |
| **Seconds from winning the ball to the shot** (`buildup_seconds`) | How quickly a move became a shot. | Seconds from the recovery that began the move to the shot. | Quick moves catch the defence unset. | Time from winning the ball to shooting. | `goal`, `big_chance` |
| **Move began with a counter-press** (`counter_press_in_move`) | The goal started from winning it straight back. | 1 when the possession that ended in the shot began with a counter-press recovery. | It connects pressing to the goals it creates. | Whether the move began by winning the ball back at once. | `goal`, `big_chance` |
| **Long balls in the move** (`long_balls_in_move`) | The move used a long pass. | Completed passes of 30 m or more in the possession that ended in the shot. | It shows when a goal came from going direct. | Long passes inside the move that ended in the shot. | `goal`, `big_chance` |
| **Expected threat (xT)** (`xt`) | How much closer to scoring a team moved the ball. | Sum of xT added by completed passes and carries in the window (Karun Singh's grid, fitted on simulator seeds 0-199). | Passes and carries that move the ball into valuable places create the chances. | How much closer to scoring each team moved the ball with passes and carries. | `zone_damage`, `momentum_swing` |
| **Back-line height** (`line_height`) | How far up the pitch the defence stands. | Mean depth of the back line out of possession, metres from own goal. | A high line squeezes the pitch; a deep line invites pressure. | How far from its own goal the defence stands without the ball. | `block_drop` |
| **Back-line height with the ball** (`line_height_in`) | How far up the defence stands when attacking. | Mean depth of the back line in possession, metres from own goal. | It shows how much a team commits forward. | How far from its own goal the defence stands when the team has the ball. | head-to-head only |
| **Gap, defence to midfield** (`gap_def_mid`) | How much space sits between defence and midfield. | Metres between the back line and the next line, out of possession. | A wide gap is space for the opponent to receive in. | Distance between the defence and the midfield without the ball. | `lines_stretched` |
| **Gap, midfield to forwards** (`gap_mid_att`) | How much space sits between midfield and attack. | Metres between the last two lines, out of possession. | A wide gap means the forwards are cut off from the team. | Distance between the midfield and the forwards without the ball. | head-to-head only |
| **Team length** (`team_length`) | How stretched the team is from back to front. | Deepest to highest outfield player, metres. | A long team is easy to play through. | Distance from the deepest to the highest outfield player. | `lines_stretched` |
| **Team width** (`team_width`) | How wide the team spreads. | Spread of the outfield players across the pitch, metres. | Width stretches the opponent sideways. | Distance between the widest outfield players. | head-to-head only |
| **Compactness** (`compactness`) | How tightly the team packs. | Convex-hull area of the outfield players, m2. Smaller is tighter. | A compact team leaves no gaps between the lines. | The area the outfield players cover. Smaller is tighter. | head-to-head only |
| **Shape without the ball** (`formation_out`) | How the team lines up when defending. | Players per line, back to front, from average positions out of possession. | It shows the defensive plan. | How the team lines up when defending, read from player positions. | head-to-head only |
| **Shape with the ball** (`formation_in`) | How the team lines up when attacking. | Players per line, back to front, from average positions in possession. | A change of shape is a change of plan. | How the team lines up when it has the ball, read from player positions. | `shape_change` |
| **Line-breaking passes** (`line_breaks`) | Passes that cut through the opponent's lines. | Completed passes ending at least 10 % closer to goal that cross a line's depth inside its lateral span (outermost players +- 2 m). | Passing through the lines is what turns possession into chances. | Completed passes that crossed a line of opponents through its span, not round the side. | `line_breakers` |
| **Opponents bypassed** (`packing`) | How many players one pass takes out of the game. | Opponent outfield players between the passer's x and the receiver's x on the pass's frame. | More players bypassed means a more damaging pass. | Opponents left behind by completed passes. | `most_dangerous_pass` |
| **Received between the lines** (`between_lines`) | Getting the ball in the pockets of space. | Completed passes received between two consecutive opponent lines, inside their span and 3 m from every opponent. | Receiving between the lines is how a block gets turned. | Passes received in the space between lines of the opponent, away from every defender. | `line_breakers` |
| **Passes beating the defence** (`line_breaks_back`) | Passes through the back line. | Line-breaking passes whose crossing is of the back line. | Beating the last line leaves a runner one-on-one with the keeper. | Completed passes that crossed the opponent's back line. | head-to-head only |
| **Passes beating midfield** (`line_breaks_mid`) | Passes through the midfield. | Line-breaking passes whose crossing is of the midfield line. | It splits the midfield from the attackers' point of view. | Completed passes that crossed the opponent's midfield line. | head-to-head only |
| **Passes beating the forward line** (`line_breaks_fwd`) | Passes past the first line of the press. | Line-breaking passes whose crossing is of the forward line. | It is how a team plays out of the press. | Completed passes that crossed the opponent's forward line. | head-to-head only |
| **Received between midfield and defence** (`between_mid_def`) | Receiving in the pocket behind the midfield. | Receptions in space between the opponent's midfield and back line. | It is the most dangerous pocket in front of the defence. | Passes received between the opponent's midfield and defence. | head-to-head only |
| **Received between forwards and midfield** (`between_fwd_mid`) | Receiving behind the first line of the press. | Receptions in space between the opponent's forward and midfield lines. | It beats the first line of the press. | Passes received between the opponent's forwards and midfield. | head-to-head only |
| **Box entries** (`box_entries`) | Getting the ball into the penalty area. | Completed passes and carries that end in the area from outside it. | It is the last step before a shot. | Passes and carries that reached the penalty area. | head-to-head only |
| **Zone 14 entries** (`zone14_entries`) | Getting the ball into the space in front of the box. | Completed passes and carries that end in zone 14 from outside it. | Zone 14 is where chances are created from. | Passes and carries that reached the central pocket just outside the box. | head-to-head only |
| **Half-space entries** (`halfspace_entries`) | Getting the ball into the lanes between centre and wing. | Completed passes and carries that end in a final-third half-space from outside it. | The half-spaces are the hardest places to defend. | Passes and carries that reached the lanes between the centre and the wings. | head-to-head only |
| **Threat down the left** (`xt_share_left`) | How much of the danger came down the left. | Share of the team's positive xT that ended in the left third of the width. | Threat that goes one way can be defended one way. | Share of a team's threat that came down the left. | `zone_damage` |
| **Threat through the middle** (`xt_share_centre`) | How much of the danger came through the middle. | Share of the team's positive xT that ended in the central third. | Most threat goes through the middle anyway. | Share of a team's threat that came through the middle. | `zone_damage` |
| **Threat down the right** (`xt_share_right`) | How much of the danger came down the right. | Share of the team's positive xT that ended in the right third of the width. | Threat that goes one way can be defended one way. | Share of a team's threat that came down the right. | `zone_damage` |
| **xT added by the pass** (`pass_xt_added`) | How much one pass changed the danger. | xT of the end cell minus xT of the start cell, for one completed pass. | It singles out the pass that opened the game. | How much closer to scoring one pass moved the ball. | `most_dangerous_pass` |
| **Opponents the pass bypassed** (`pass_packing`) | How many defenders one pass left behind. | Opponent outfield players between the start x and end x of one completed pass. | It measures how much of the defence one pass beat. | Opponents left behind by one pass. | `most_dangerous_pass` |
| **Successful presses** (`press_wins`) | Pressing that won the ball. | A team's pressures followed within 5 s by that team winning the ball (tackle won or interception); one win per press. | It tells effective pressing from busy pressing. | Pressures that were followed by winning the ball within five seconds. | head-to-head only |
| **Press success** (`press_success`) | How often pressing pays off. | Successful presses / pressures. | A high rate means the press is organised, not just frantic. | Share of pressures that won the ball within five seconds. | head-to-head only |
| **Ball wins** (`regains`) | Times a team took the ball off the other. | Tackles won plus interceptions. | It is the raw material of a counter. | Tackles won and interceptions. | head-to-head only |
| **Wide regains** (`regains_wide_share`) | Whether the ball is won out wide or in the middle. | Share of ball wins made in the outer thirds of the width. | A team that wins it wide is steering the opponent to the touchline: a pressing trap. | Share of ball wins made in the wide lanes. | head-to-head only |
| **Escapes through the press** (`press_escapes_short`) | Playing out of the press with a pass. | Completed build-up passes (start x <= 63) that break the opponent's forward line or are received between forwards and midfield. | It is the short way out of a press. | Build-up passes that got past the first line of the opponent's press. | head-to-head only |
| **Escapes over the press** (`press_escapes_long`) | Going long to escape the press. | Completed build-up passes of 30 m or more that did not already beat the first line. | It is the long way out of a press. | Long build-up passes that flew over the opponent's press. | head-to-head only |
| **Press escapes** (`press_escapes`) | Getting out of the press. | Escapes through (short) plus escapes over (long) the press. | A team that keeps escaping makes pressing pointless. | Build-up passes that beat the first line of the press, short or long. | `press_escape` |
| **Press-escape rate** (`press_escape_rate`) | How often build-up gets out of the press. | Press escapes / the team's build-up passes (start x <= 63). | It says how reliable a team's way out is. | Share of build-up passes that beat the first line of the press. | `press_escape` |
| **Big chances** (`big_chances`) | The clear chances a team had. | Shots with xG >= 0.3. | Big chances are where games are won. | Shots that were worth at least 0.3 expected goals. | head-to-head only |
| **xG per shot** (`xg_per_shot`) | How good the average chance was. | Expected goals of the shots / number of shots. | It separates quality from quantity. | Average expected goals per shot. | head-to-head only |
| **Finishing vs xG** (`finishing`) | Scoring more or fewer goals than the chances deserved. | Goals minus expected goals. | It marks luck and finishing: above zero is clinical, below is wasteful. | Goals scored minus the goals the chances were worth. | head-to-head only |
| **Shots from cut-backs** (`shots_cutback`) | Chances made by pulling the ball back. | Shots whose assist starts by the goal line (x >= 94) in a wide lane and goes back 3 m or more into the box. | Cut-backs are among the most valuable chances. | Shots set up by a pass pulled back from the by-line. | head-to-head only |
| **Shots from crosses** (`shots_cross`) | Chances made by crossing. | Shots whose assist starts in a wide lane of the final third and ends in the box. | Crosses are common and worth less than cut-backs. | Shots set up by a cross into the area. | head-to-head only |
| **Shots from through balls** (`shots_through`) | Chances made by splitting the defence. | Shots whose assist breaks the opponent's back line. | Through balls get the shooter behind the defence. | Shots set up by a pass through the defence. | head-to-head only |
| **Long shots** (`shots_long`) | Efforts from distance. | Shots from 25 m or more that were not set up by a cut-back, cross or through ball. | Long shots are a sign a team could not break the defence down. | Shots taken from 25 m or more. | head-to-head only |
| **Hub share** (`hub_share`) | How much play runs through one player. | The most-involved player's passes made or received / the team's completed passes. | A hub is a strength and a target. | Share of a team's completed passes the busiest player made or received. | head-to-head only |
| **Player involvement** (`player_involvement`) | How much play runs through a player. | Completed passes the player made or received / the team's completed passes in the window. Shares add up to 2. | It finds the player a pass network is built around. | Share of a team's completed passes a player made or received. | `hub_player` |
| **Distance per player** (`distance_per_min`) | How much ground the team covers. | Straight-line metres between freeze-frame checkpoints (>= 4 s apart), per outfield player per minute. Not continuous tracking. | A fall late in the match is fatigue. | Metres an outfield player covers per minute, measured from positions at ball events. | `physical_drop` |
| **Sprints** (`sprints`) | How many full-speed runs. | Checkpoint legs at an average of 25 km/h or more. | Sprinting is the first thing tired legs lose. | Runs at 25 km/h or more, measured between checkpoints. | head-to-head only |
| **High-speed runs** (`high_speed_runs`) | How many fast runs. | Checkpoint legs at an average of 20 km/h or more. | It shows intensity beyond sprints. | Runs at 20 km/h or more, measured between checkpoints. | head-to-head only |
| **Top speed** (`top_speed_kmh`) | The fastest run. | Largest checkpoint-leg speed, km/h. | It is a ceiling, not an average. | Fastest speed measured between checkpoints, km/h. | head-to-head only |

## Player cards and the rating

Served by `GET /matches/{seed}/players` (`pulse/ratings.py`).

| Figure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Rating (1-10)** (`rating`) | 5.2 plus visible parts: xT, ball wins, key passes and xG created, finishing, minus losses. The breakdown is on the card. |
| **Involvement** (`involvement`) | Completed passes the player made or received. |
| **xT added** (`xt`) | Expected threat added by the player's completed passes and carries. |
| **Progressive actions** (`progressive`) | Progressive passes plus carries of 10 m or more towards goal. |
| **Line-breaking passes** (`line_breaks`) | Completed passes through at least one opponent line. |
| **Key passes** (`key_passes`) | Completed passes that found a shooter, played right before the shot. |
| **xA** (`xa`) | Expected goals of the shots the player's key passes created. |
| **Ball wins** (`wins`) | Tackles won and interceptions. |
| **Distance** (`distance_m`) | Metres between freeze-frame checkpoints. Not continuous tracking. |
| **Top speed** (`top_speed_kmh`) | Fastest checkpoint leg, km/h. |
| **Sprints** (`sprints`) | Checkpoint legs at 25 km/h or more. |

Rating = 5.2 + 3.0 x xT added + 0.08 x ball wins + 0.25 x key passes + 2.0 x xA + 0.8 x goals + 1.0 x own xG - 0.03 x incomplete passes - 0.10 x lost tackles, clipped to 1-10. The weights were set on simulator seeds 0-19 (mean outfield player 6.3, best of a match about 8.7); they are a stated convention, not an external benchmark.
